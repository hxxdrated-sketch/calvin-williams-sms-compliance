Calvin Williams Trading Alerts

Calvin Williams Trading Alerts is an SMS notification program operated by Calvin Williams. It sends automated, non-marketing text messages about trading activity in Calvin Williams's own Alpaca brokerage account, such as order fills and price alerts.

The alerts are sent by Calvin Williams's own trading software through Twilio. Messages go only to mobile numbers that have opted in using the consent form on the Opt-In page.
