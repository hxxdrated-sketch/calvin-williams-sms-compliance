Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Calvin Williams ("we," "us") handles information for the Calvin Williams Trading Alerts SMS program.

Information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number you enter on the Opt-In page when you agree to receive SMS alerts, along with a record of your consent.

How we use it

Your phone number is used only to send the trading alerts described in the Terms and Conditions and to respond to HELP and STOP requests.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. The only exception is our SMS delivery provider (Twilio), which processes your number solely to deliver the messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
